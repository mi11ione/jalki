# Jälki v15 · method note (one page)

*Synthetic pack only: every figure comes from the organisers' synthetic challenge data, and none is Apetit's real emissions. Roles, approvals and supplier offers are simulated. Each figure is the string the v8 build showed, checked against Jälki v15's own registry (`python3 stages/pj-release15/verify15.py`); where v15 shows a figure, its source opens from it in the app.*

**Why this shape.** At our stand the Apetit team asked for “Something ready to use.” and said trust needs “A good documentation. And traceability”. So the first view, *The promise*, answers what the 2032 promise commits the company to and costs, and every figure in Jälki opens its rule, script and source records. [page]

**The claim.** On the recommended route, FLAG is 37,100–45,500 t short of the 2032 target at central lever bands, counting physical cuts only; the first view says *The 2032 promise: €1.75M a year, and still 37,100 t short.* The price is the recommended route's cost a year, annual capital charge included. With every catalogue lever at its high band it is still 26,981 t short. 2032 (SBTi V2) and 2035 (V1.3.1) need the same 5,342 t of FLAG cut a year, so 2035 is only longer. L11 (crops moved off peat) earns no credit until the same parcels are retired or rewetted. [P]

**The inventory.** One pipeline builds the 2025 inventory from the raw feeds: cleaned activity × factor × unit conversion. A second pipeline, built blind, recomputes Scope 1, Scope 2 and category 1 and scores 94.44 on the pack's public 2023 key. Corrections of 10 t or more (107 of them) are decided one by one before signing. A rejection may be signed; replay then shows the review check failing. Validated unit and duplicate fixes (1,275,763 t of raw-feed typos) are not up for rejection. [P][page]

**The base-year meter.** A drained peat field is an old bog ditched for crops that keeps releasing CO₂; ours alone move the 2025 base year past the line, as an acquisition would. The peat method moves the base 6.02–6.95% (central 6.83%), past the 5% recalculation line. The honest floor, soil map only with nothing fitted, is 6.02%, and 5.01–6.02% across the 8 bases and denominators tested (32 t over the line at the lowest). In the weakest historical year the map-only reading is 4.76–5.22% and straddles the line. Week-one falsifier: 20 area-weighted random draws on mapped peat; 7 or more mineral results re-run the soil comparison before signing (false alarm 0.67% at twelve percent mineral, power 94.2% at half, assuming independent draws read correctly). [F][page]

**The route and its money.** The recommended route costs €1.75M a year with €3.02M of investment, from catalogue capex and opex with capital at an assumed 8% (the pack gives no rate). L01, L07, L14 and L22 stay out: the pack does not show their capacity. L07 rests on about 7,320 MWh of Swedish biogas, while Bjuv's 2025 meters show natural gas only, so own operations end 791–1,006 t short in 2032. HVO is the largest cost, €622k (35%), and buys 0 t of FLAG; pausing it widens the other value-chain gap by 3,126–3,276 t. [P]

**Bought rapeseed.** Carrying the gap, seed must fall from 1.13 t/t to 0.72–0.78 t/t (FLAG part only), cleaner than the cleanest origin in the library (0.88 t/t). At full scale that costs €4.9–11.4M a year, 2.8–6.5× the route, a premium of 9.9–21.8% on the seed price across both scenarios. So the decision is a pilot capped at €848k a year, floor 1,696 t (4.6% of the gap), ceiling €250 per verified tonne. *Assumptions:* catalogue prices hold at scale; year one follows the catalogue's own supplier ramp; the floor is half of central delivery. No supplier offer exists yet. [P]

**The board's rapeseed decision, measured.** 167 of 230 contract turnip-rape farms (73%) sent a 2025 field record. The highest- and lowest-yield quarters (41 farms each) put about the same nitrogen on a hectare (95 kg N/ha and 87 kg N/ha); the top quarter harvests 1.68 t/ha against 1.09 t/ha, so it uses 55 kg N/t against 81 kg N/t, and on mineral soil its tonnes carry 0.61 t CO₂e/t against 0.78 t CO₂e/t. Pessimistic re-run, thirds on mineral-soil farms only: 1.36× the nitrogen and 1.30× the FLAG per tonne. Self-reported and one season: it shows the spread the programme can work on, not what any farm will achieve. So decision F measures the decision instead of judging it: parcel ID, yield and nitrogen rate on the 2027 grower form, reported per farm every season. [R]

**Whose numbers the base is made of.** The library writes 62.5% of the base, suppliers 22.6%, our farms 11.3%. One source writes 35.3%; a 14.16% revision of it moves 10,026 t of FLAG, 1.88 years of the cut, with no recalculation forced: a revision is not a reduction. 5 rapeseed suppliers' own footprints moved 11.0–48.5% in a year against a year-one cut of 7.7–9.0%; across all 7 valid pairs the median is 11.1%. So the pilot pays on field records (nitrogen rate, yield, area), never on a lower footprint. [W]

**Nature and the frameworks.** Our 10 biggest CO₂ farms send only 2.5% of the phosphorus. The lakes need a different list: CA12 first. The contract clause for lake catchments puts a buffer strip on every contract parcel without one that lies within 100 m of water in a catchment below good status: 150 parcels on 115 farms, about 175 kg less phosphorus to water a year, 4.1% of the contract total, on model A's buffer-strip coefficient; at half that effect, 87 kg. [C] Framework questions are answered from the pack's GHG Protocol, SBTi and SBTN texts: 7 answers, 23 quoted passages, each found word for word in the file it cites, each an AI draft that waits for an expert to approve it. [N][A]

**Limits.** The headline gap rests partly on an uncited constant for L16; with L16 at zero it is 39,380–47,937 t. The seal shows that nothing changed after signing; it is not a signature, and roles are not authenticated. HVO's cost has two sizings in the pack, not yet reconciled. Climate euros and the pea share come from yield model B, which lost its forward test to model A; read them as an order of magnitude. Climate figures are 2041–60 scenario spreads, not forecasts. A model-run screen test is not a usability test.

**Checks.** The facts checker runs first: 127 of 128 files and 15 of 15 facts; the one missing file is the organisers' non-CC-BY sample workbook, withheld by design and read by no stage. The organisers' scorer, unchanged, gives A1 97.97, A2 98.27, D1 71.17 and E 100.0 on the public development keys, and C1 56.82 and C2 24.27 as development replays; none is a hidden-test score. A clean copy of this package rebuilds the app byte for byte (`python3 check_rebuild.py`).
